Robotics sensing · ROS 2

Multi-Sensor Data Integration

Northeastern University · March 2025

I collected IMU and GPS measurements with ROS 2 and compared how different filtering choices affected heading and velocity estimates.

Heading from multiple sensors

I calibrated magnetometer readings and combined their long-term heading with short-term gyroscope motion using a complementary filter. ROS 2 bags preserved the sensor streams for repeatable analysis.

Velocity and bias

I compared velocity inferred from IMU acceleration with GPS measurements, then evaluated filtering and bias correction to reduce inertial drift.
