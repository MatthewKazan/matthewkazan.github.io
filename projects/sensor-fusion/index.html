Robotics sensing · ROS 2

ROS 2 Sensor Fusion

Northeastern University · March 2025

I recorded a drive with an IMU and GPS, then replayed the data to see how well I could recover the car’s heading and velocity.

Heading from multiple sensors

I calibrated the magnetometer and used a complementary filter to combine its long-term heading with the gyroscope’s short-term response. I saved raw sensor streams in ROS 2 bags so I could compare filters on the same drive.

Velocity and bias

I compared velocity inferred from IMU acceleration with GPS measurements, then evaluated filtering and bias correction to reduce inertial drift.
