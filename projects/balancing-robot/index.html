Reinforcement learning · Robot simulation

Self-Balancing Robot

Northeastern University · April 2024

I modeled a two-wheel inverted-pendulum robot and trained a Proximal Policy Optimization agent to balance it in simulation.

From CAD to a training environment

I used Onshape and PyBullet to define the robot and its dynamics. A Gym interface exposes orientation and velocity observations and converts the policy’s output to wheel commands.

Policy training and evaluation

I trained PPO with TensorFlow Agents and compared it with a neuroevolution baseline. The experiments measured balance duration, drift, and control smoothness while testing reward and simulation settings.
