Wireless Fencing Scoring

Personal project · December 2020

I prototyped wearable devices that register épée touches and a Swift app that displays and edits the score.

Hardware and firmware repository

Touch detection hardware

Each device connects to the fencer’s standard body cord. I designed the sensing circuit, wrote microcontroller firmware, and modeled a 3D-printed enclosure.

Scorekeeping interface

The Swift app displays and edits scores through Firebase Realtime Database. Bringing the hardware and app together required debugging the sensor input, timing, and communication paths.
