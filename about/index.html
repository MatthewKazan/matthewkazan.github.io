About me

I’m Matt. I work on warehouse robots at Locus, and I spend a lot of my own project time teaching cameras to estimate how they moved.

What I’m building now

At work I use motion capture and simulation to understand where localization and positioning errors come from. In my visual pose research I get to work on the full loop, from deriving the solver and rendering the training data to training a model and checking what it actually learned.
